# Skills at a Glance

You do not need to memorize these skills. Tell the agent what you are trying to
do and share what you already have. It will usually choose the right skill.

Skills marked **ask for it** start only when you name them. They change the
shape of the conversation, so the agent will not enter them by accident.

## Collaboration

| Skill | What you bring | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `product-co-design` **(ask for it)** | An idea, audience, constraints, or even a half-formed problem | A pressure-tested product concept, key choices, risks, and next step |
| `interview-me` **(ask for it)** | A topic you want to think through | A focused one-question-at-a-time interview and a useful synthesis |
| `low-friction-collaboration` **(ask for it)** | A complex task or conversation | The same depth of work, explained more clearly and kept easy to resume |
| `multi-step-governance` **(ask for it)** | An end-to-end outcome with dependent stages | An authorized durable frontier, evidence, and next task shape |
| `product-research` | A product question, audience, and decision to inform | Evidence-backed findings, limitations, and decision implications |
| `domain-modeling` | Domain language, examples, code, or existing rules | Clear terms, relationships, invariants, and unresolved contradictions |
| `decision-mapping` | A destination with important choices still open | A map of resolved and open decisions, dependencies, and the next decision |
| `portable-handoff` | Current work, its recipient, and what happens next | A compact, redacted handoff with evidence, risks, and one next action |
| `parallel-subagents` | Two or more genuinely independent assignments | Concurrent evidence gathered into one verified result |
| `second-opinion` | A consequential plan, diagnosis, conclusion, or diff | An independent critique and a reasoned response to its findings |

## Engineering

| Skill | What you bring | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `technical-research` | A bounded software or architecture question | Cited findings, reproducible checks, tradeoffs, and remaining uncertainty |
| `implementation-spec` | A settled goal, constraints, evidence, and non-goals | A global implementation contract and the current actionable frontier |
| `work-slicing` | An accepted implementation contract | The next verifiable slice, its dependencies, and its completion condition |
| `large-codebase-change` | A requested change in a large existing repository | A small, idiomatic, tested, PR-ready diff |
| `prototype-builder` | A core idea and the behavior it should demonstrate | A runnable vertical slice, basic verification, and clearly named shortcuts |
| `diagnosing-bugs` | The symptom, expected behavior, and reproduction clues | A reproducer, tested hypotheses, root cause, and—when requested—a verified fix |
| `code-review` | A fixed diff, intended behavior, and any prior review findings | Evidence-gated findings, remediation reconciliation, and a pass or corrective disposition |

## AI

| Skill | What you bring | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ai-pipeline` **(ask for it)** | An AI goal, available evidence, and practical constraints | An initial end-to-end pipeline plan and recommended next skills |
| `ai-data-preparation` | Raw data, its meaning, and the desired format | Clean analysis-ready data or a precise preparation plan, with provenance |
| `ai-insight-development` | Interpretable evidence and a domain question | Supported insights, alternate explanations, and candidate signals |
| `ai-representation-workbench` | Evidence or a formal domain plus information constraints | Validated features, states, embeddings, knowledge, or heuristics |
| `ai-problem-modeling` | An objective, evidence, constraints, and failure costs | A formal task, candidate approaches, baselines, metrics, and experiment plan |
| `ai-performance` | A candidate solution, data, evaluation rules, and compute limits | Built or trained artifacts, comparative results, diagnostics, and a decision |

## Writing

| Skill | What you bring | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `humanizer` | A writing goal or draft, audience, evidence, and desired voice | Natural long-form prose or a faithful revision that preserves meaning |
| `blogpost-writing` | A post idea, source material, and author-owned experience | VitePress-ready blog or CTF writeup Markdown in N00bcak's voice |
