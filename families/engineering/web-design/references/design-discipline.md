# Web Design Discipline

Use these tests when proposing directions and when inspecting the implemented
result. They are diagnostic questions, not a required aesthetic or component
quota.

## Earn the Structure

- Derive page sequence from the content or task journey. Do not default to a
  navigation bar, centered hero, paired calls to action, feature grid, social
  proof, pricing, and final call to action.
- Use a container only for a genuinely separate object or interaction. Prefer
  alignment, proximity, rules, lists, bands, and whitespace over nested cards,
  bento grids, and pills around ordinary labels.
- Keep a coherent spacing system while making optical exceptions for different
  content roles. Articles, captions, tables, heroes, and control surfaces do not
  require identical padding or rhythm.
- Open operational software on its working surface. Do not wrap a calendar,
  editor, dashboard, or other repeated-action tool in unrequested marketing
  theatre.

## Earn the Visual Language

- Derive color from the subject's materials, history, audience, or environment.
  Reject category shortcuts such as violet for AI, navy and green for finance,
  beige serif treatments for wellness, or black and acid green for developer
  tools unless the actual context supports them.
- Treat typography as composition: measure, line breaks, width, optical size,
  punctuation, weight contrast, hierarchy, and relationship to imagery. Font
  selection alone is not a typographic direction.
- Recede secondary information without making it illegible or undiscoverable.
  Do not simulate quality with faint gray text, translucent controls, glass
  surfaces, or borders visible only on hover.
- Prefer clear text over an icon. Use icons or illustrations only when their
  meaning or subject-specific visual language justifies them; do not fill empty
  corners with generic symbols in rounded squares.

## Earn Information and Motion

- Every graph, status mark, number, orbit, grid, activity item, and decorative
  line must encode real content or fixture state. Remove decoration that poses
  as evidence or system activity.
- Use numbering only for actual order. Use charts only when they answer a real
  question. Never invent analytics to make a page appear complete.
- Choreograph at most the moments the interface needs. Motion should explain a
  change, preserve continuity, reveal causality, or support navigation—not add
  repeated fades, lifts, glows, and drifting backgrounds.

## Earn Completeness

- Design applicable loading, empty, error, selected, destructive, overflow,
  keyboard, and responsive states before calling a surface implemented.
- Ensure toggles, filters, navigation, and other controls change real frontend
  state. Clearly identify fixture-backed behavior and missing production seams.
- Adapt information priority and interaction mechanics across widths. Do not
  call mobile complete merely because desktop columns stack vertically.
- For reading experiences, derive measure, pacing, references, captions, and
  navigation from the actual text. Avoid automatic art-magazine styling such as
  cream paper, giant serif titles, editorial numerals, and oversized whitespace.

## Final Test

For each conspicuous choice, finish this sentence:

> This form is appropriate because the content, audience, material, state, or
> interaction requires or strongly supports it.

If the reason is only familiarity, trend, market category, empty space, or a
desire to look designed, revise or remove the choice.
